Get in touch

Found a bug, stuck somewhere, or have an idea? Email jaxonfn@gmail.com. It helps to say what you were doing, the year and season in the game, and which iPhone you have. A screenshot is even better.

Common questions

How do I start a new life? Open the ⋯ menu at the top of the game screen and choose Abandon run, or finish your current one. Your Codex and family record are kept.

The horse-breeders are locked. They open once you have played one game to its end: your family's line runs out, or your last soldier serves out his years.

Can I turn the music off? Yes: Settings → Music and sound. The iPhone's silent switch also silences the game.

The text is too small or too large. Settings → Text size. "Match my iPhone" follows the text size in your iPhone's own settings.

Will I lose my game if I delete the app? Yes. Saves are kept only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them.
