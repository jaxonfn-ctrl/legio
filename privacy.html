Legio

Privacy policy · last updated 26 November 2026

In short

Legio collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never sends anything over the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

The game keeps a few things on your device so it can work: your saved lives and family record, the Codex entries you have found, and your settings (text size, music and sound, haptics). They are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to me or to anyone else. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, Apple may include them in that backup, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What I don't do

Legio does not collect your name, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. It contains no advertising and no third-party code that collects data, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

If you email me

If you write to me for support, I will see your email address and whatever you send, and use it only to reply. I will not share it.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: jaxonfn@gmail.com.
